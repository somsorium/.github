# somsorium

**Private intelligence. Public ambition.**

We build software, explore ideas, and create worlds.

somsorium brings together curiosity about new technologies and the desire to shape things ourselves. We explore artificial intelligence, shared knowledge, and digital spaces where people can experiment, discover, and work together.

## What we’re working on

Our projects range from locally run AI assistance with **smolit** to tools for collaborative research and connected communities. Open technologies, control over our own tools, and a thoughtful approach to data and resources guide our work.

With **Nova Protocol (working title)**, we’re also developing a cooperative open-world sci-fi game. At its heart is the discovery of a mysterious energy that makes travel to other planets possible. Exploration, survival, crafting, and automation unfold alongside the mystery of a world whose connections gradually reveal themselves.

Across these projects, we share a fascination with understanding systems, exploring possibilities, and building things together.

## How we think

We like technology we can question, understand, and improve. We work with open approaches, learn through experiments, and think beyond individual applications.

Our projects are at different stages of development. Here, we share code, concepts, and the questions we’re working through.

## Curious?

Whether you’re interested in AI, open software, or unfamiliar worlds, we welcome people who ask questions, contribute ideas, and explore alongside us.
